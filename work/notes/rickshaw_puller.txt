Rickshaw puller, the unsung hero of bustling streets, starts his day before sunrise, preparing his sturdy wooden cart for the relentless traffic. He checks the wheels, tightens the bolts, and ensures the canvas cover is intact against the morning drizzle. With a deep breath, he grips the pull rope, feeling the weight of countless passengers' hopes and destinations.

His muscles, honed by years of relentless pulling, flex as he navigates narrow alleys and crowded avenues. The rhythmic creak of the wheels becomes a metronome to the city's heartbeat, echoing through markets and residential lanes.

Each passenger offers a brief story: a vendor heading to the market, a student rushing to class, an elderly woman carrying groceries. The puller listens, nods, and adjusts his pace, embodying the pulse of urban life.

Rain pours, yet he persists, his determination unshaken. The slick roads test his balance, but his experience guides him safely through puddles and slippery turns.

Evening descends, and the city lights flicker on. The puller’s silhouette against neon signs reflects a day of tireless service, his silhouette a testament to endurance.

He returns home, his cart resting, muscles sore yet satisfied. The day’s earnings are modest, but the pride of sustaining livelihoods fuels his spirit.

In the quiet of night, he dreams of better roads, safer conditions, and recognition for his indispensable role in the urban tapestry.

The rickshaw puller remains a vital thread, weaving together the fabric of daily commutes, embodying resilience, humility, and the relentless drive of the human spirit.
